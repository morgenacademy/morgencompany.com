Isometric layered paper-craft diorama floating as a small rounded island on a plain
solid #0C0818 background with a soft contact shadow beneath it. Light matte cardstock
and folded origami paper, clean die-cut edges, subtle drop shadows between layers,
tilt-shift miniature look, smooth graceful studio lighting. The world is bathed in a
soft black-purple glow: deep #0C0818 shadows, rich purple #5B2D8E ambient light and
gentle lavender #9B6FCF highlights rising between the paper layers. A vivid
yellow-green #D8FE56 accent is used very sparingly, only on small details such as tiny
lanterns, little flags and one glowing round landmark dot. Highly detailed, centered
composition, absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.

Subject: the interior of the miniature paper theater, its domed roof lifted away so
the room opens up like a dollhouse. A warm intimate stage with layered paper curtains
and a glowing arch of tiny lights, a single paper speaker standing mid story on stage,
and curved rows of paper audience seats filled with listening figures, some leaning
forward. Soft spotlight cone made of translucent paper, drifting paper dust in the
beam. Backdrop on stage shows only abstract rays and simple geometric shapes,
strictly no readable text, no letters, no text-like scribbles. Rich purple velvet
tones with lavender highlights, warm golden stage light, sparse yellow-green accents
on the arch lights. The lit speaker on stage is the focal point, centered in the
frame with a little headroom above.
