Isometric layered paper-craft diorama floating as a small rounded island on a plain
solid #0C0818 background with a soft contact shadow beneath it. Light matte cardstock
and folded origami paper, clean die-cut edges, subtle drop shadows between layers,
tilt-shift miniature look, smooth graceful studio lighting. The world is bathed in a
soft black-purple glow: deep #0C0818 shadows, rich purple #5B2D8E ambient light and
gentle lavender #9B6FCF highlights rising between the paper layers. A vivid
yellow-green #D8FE56 accent is used very sparingly, only on small details such as tiny
lanterns, little flags and one glowing round landmark dot. Highly detailed, centered
composition, absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.

Subject: a close, intimate corner of the miniature paper campus garden: the sculpture
garden of finished projects. Three low stone pedestals in a gentle arc on a garden
path, each carrying a tiny finished diorama-within-the-diorama under a clear glass
dome: a miniature paper webshop storefront, a miniature paper office with tiny
screens, and a miniature paper factory line, each lit by its own small lantern.
Purple paper flowers and hedges around the pedestals, a fragment of the winding path
in front. Warm museum-like pools of light on each dome, cozy purple ambient glow
with lavender highlights, sparse yellow-green accents on the lantern flames. The
three glass domes are the focal point, centered in the frame with a little headroom
above.
