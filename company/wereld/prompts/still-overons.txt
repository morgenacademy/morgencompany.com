Isometric layered paper-craft diorama floating as a small rounded island on a plain
solid #0C0818 background with a soft contact shadow beneath it. Light matte cardstock
and folded origami paper, clean die-cut edges, subtle drop shadows between layers,
tilt-shift miniature look, smooth graceful studio lighting. The world is bathed in a
soft black-purple glow: deep #0C0818 shadows, rich purple #5B2D8E ambient light and
gentle lavender #9B6FCF highlights rising between the paper layers. A vivid
yellow-green #D8FE56 accent is used very sparingly, only on small details such as tiny
lanterns, little flags and one glowing round landmark dot. Highly detailed, centered
composition, absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.

Subject: a warm, quiet corner of the miniature paper campus garden at the campfire
circle: folded-paper flames glowing warmly in a small stone ring, surrounded by tree
stump seats. A handful of paper figures sit together around the fire, relaxed and
mid conversation, one leaning forward telling a story, one holding a tiny paper mug.
Soft firelight dancing on their faces and on the purple paper plants and rocks
around them, a fragment of the winding path behind. Cozy purple ambient glow with
lavender highlights, the warm orange fire as the heart of the scene, sparse
yellow-green accents on a small lantern nearby. The campfire with its circle of
figures is the focal point, centered in the frame with a little headroom above.
