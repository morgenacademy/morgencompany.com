Isometric layered paper-craft diorama floating as a small rounded island on a plain
solid #0C0818 background with a soft contact shadow beneath it. Light matte cardstock
and folded origami paper, clean die-cut edges, subtle drop shadows between layers,
tilt-shift miniature look, smooth graceful studio lighting. The world is bathed in a
soft black-purple glow: deep #0C0818 shadows, rich purple #5B2D8E ambient light and
gentle lavender #9B6FCF highlights rising between the paper layers. A vivid
yellow-green #D8FE56 accent is used very sparingly, only on small details such as tiny
lanterns, little flags and one glowing round landmark dot. Highly detailed, centered
composition, absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.

Subject: the interior of the miniature paper workshop hall, its roof lifted away so the
room opens up like a dollhouse. An open makers floor: sturdy wooden paper workbenches,
tiny paper tools hanging on a pegboard wall, small folded-paper machines with visible
gears, and a miniature paper conveyor line carrying little parcels. Paper figures work
side by side: two assembling a small machine together, one adjusting the conveyor, one
pointing at a wall board with abstract flow shapes, pure geometry, strictly no
readable text, no letters, no text-like scribbles anywhere. Warm inviting maker
atmosphere: cozy purple ambient light with lavender highlights on the paper edges,
tiny yellow-green paper lanterns near the benches. The busy shared workbench in the
middle is the focal point, centered in the frame with a little headroom above.
