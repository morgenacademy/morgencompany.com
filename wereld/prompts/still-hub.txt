Isometric layered paper-craft diorama floating as a small rounded island on a plain
solid #0C0818 background with a soft contact shadow beneath it. Light matte cardstock
and folded origami paper, clean die-cut edges, subtle drop shadows between layers,
tilt-shift miniature look, smooth graceful studio lighting. The world is bathed in a
soft black-purple glow: deep #0C0818 shadows, rich purple #5B2D8E ambient light and
gentle lavender #9B6FCF highlights rising between the paper layers. A vivid
yellow-green #D8FE56 accent is used very sparingly, only on small details such as tiny
lanterns, little flags and one glowing round landmark dot. Highly detailed, centered
composition, absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.

Subject: a miniature campus village in a lush paper garden, seen from a three-quarter
isometric angle. Four distinct paper buildings arranged around a central village
square: a stately lecture hall with tall windows revealing tiered rows of seats, an
open workshop hall with sliding doors, workbenches and hanging tools, a science lab
with a rounded glass dome and small antennas, and a cozy theater with an arched
entrance and warm marquee lights. In the middle of the square sits one large glowing
round yellow-green dot as a landmark sculpture. The lecture hall and the workshop
share one connected terrain: a single continuous winding path runs between them
through a shared courtyard, so the two clearly belong together. Paper trees, hedges,
flower beds and tiny garden lanterns fill the space between the buildings, and the
zones flow gradually into one another with no fences or hard borders. A few tiny paper
figures walk the paths. The square with its glowing dot sits exactly in the middle of
the frame with generous headroom above, nothing essential near the left or right
edges.
